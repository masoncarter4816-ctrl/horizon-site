Privacy Policy for Horizon Archive

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Horizon Archive operates an automated content generation and publishing engine that publishes educational science documentaries to YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifying information from viewers. Our application connects solely to the authenticated social media accounts of the channel administrator via official OAuth2 protocols to upload video content.

2. Use of Information

OAuth tokens obtained from YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok are encrypted and used solely for authenticating video upload requests. No user data is shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties.

3. Data Retention and Security

All authentication credentials and API keys are stored in encrypted environment storage and cloud key vaults. Temporary media rendering assets are wiped immediately following upload confirmation.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at horizonarchivehq@gmail.com.
